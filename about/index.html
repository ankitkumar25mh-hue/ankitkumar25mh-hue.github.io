About Presentation Vault

A personal presentation archive built for students who present on classroom Smartboards.

Why Presentation Vault?

As a college student, I frequently create PowerPoint presentations for class presentations. The workflow was always the same: create the presentation, upload to Google Drive, then log into Google Drive on the classroom Smartboard to present.

This process is slow, requires internet, and depends on a third-party service. Presentation Vault solves this by providing a personal, self-hosted presentation archive that works directly in the browser — no login required.

Key Features

Easy to Access

Published presentations are available through a shareable link. An internet connection is required.

Smartboard Optimized

Large touch targets, keyboard navigation, fullscreen mode designed for classroom displays.

Organized by Subject

Filter and search presentations by category — FinTech, Finance, Economics, Investment.

Presentation Mode

Dedicated viewer with slide navigation, counter, fullscreen, and keyboard shortcuts.
